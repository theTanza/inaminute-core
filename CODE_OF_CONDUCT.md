# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and contributor boundaries.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes openly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not allowed.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security reporting channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue
warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants.
Enforcement decisions will prioritize safety, proportionality, and confidentiality.
